Showcase Lab

A playable Box3D and wgpu campus. Knock down stacks and dominoes, throw moving lights, compare flat / normal-mapped / glossy panels, drive vehicles and experiment with weather.

Use the LAB entries in your action menu. Single player; experimental probes are not persistent in saved games.

Jeep circuit: 37 unarmed targets and repeated slow laps. Board, ready your personal weapon, then choose LAB: start / stop Jeep circuit. You decide when the driver starts.

Helicopter circuit: board the rifle passenger seat, ready your weapon and request departure. A dedicated pilot circles the range until you request return and landing. Passenger firing requires the default VehicleActions package.
