# @OP_TidewaterWildlife

Tidewater's humpback whale as an OFP animal, `OP_TidewaterHumpback`. Spawn it from the dev panel:
Zeus tab, *Spawn at camera aim*, **Spawn humpback whale ahead** (or `"OP_TidewaterHumpback" createVehicle`
over deep water).

## Provenance

[Tidewater](https://github.com/dgreenheck/tidewater) (an open-source WebGPU ocean demo, MIT, (c) 2026
DRG Software Solutions LLC) ships the whale in `public/models/whale/`. Its CREDITS.md: an original
procedural model generated by its authors' scripts; the proportions follow published humpback anatomy
and the side profile was traced from a NOAA Fisheries illustration (a US government work in the public
domain). No game's content is used.

`source/humpback.bin.gz` and `source/humpback_albedo.png` were exported once from Tidewater commit
`4811ba48d795197de5621985f404e765c0b7c0ef` with

```
python scripts/default-content/export_tidewater_whale.py <tidewater checkout> content/default-packs/tidewater-wildlife/source
```

which bakes Tidewater's relief map (tubercles, ventral pleats, barnacles) into the finest level.
`scripts/Build-TidewaterWildlife.ps1` (run by `Build-DefaultContent.ps1`) turns it into the model, the
skin and `config.cpp` with `scripts/default-content/generate_tidewater_whale.py`.

## What carries over

* **Body and rig**: Tidewater's three levels of detail and its 40-frame spine rig with flipper joints.
  `Whale.cpp` poses every vertex on the CPU from the frames, as Tidewater's vertex shader does.
* **Behaviour**: a port of `WhaleBrain.js`. The whale loops through the deepest water near where it
  was placed (a loop of about 700 m, 12 m of water or more where it can find it), and once a loop
  surfaces for 3-6 breaths (rise, blow, roll back under), now and then breaches or lifts a flipper and
  slaps it down, and leaves with a fluke-up dive.
* **Blows and splashes** go to the Tidewater water's spray (blow spout, breach and slap splashes).

## OFP additions

* It is a target like any vehicle (armor 350): a shot whale sounds -- dives away fast -- and a dead
  one rolls belly up and floats at the surface.
* Geometry and fire geometry are convex slices of the body in its rest pose.

Tidewater's other wildlife (fish schools, reef life, seabirds, shorebirds, crabs) is already drawn by the
renderer's Tidewater ocean and needs no pack.

## Sea sounds

`source/sound/` holds Tidewater's sea recordings: 42 field recordings from Freesound in Tidewater, all
CC0 1.0; the ones used here and their recordists are in `source/sound/CREDITS.md` (shipped in the PBO
too). `scripts/default-content/export_tidewater_sounds.py` cuts Tidewater's sprite sheets into one file
per slice and re-encodes its Opus loops as Vorbis (the engine decodes Vorbis only). Played by
`SeaSoundscape.cpp`, `Ship.cpp` and `Whale.cpp` at Tidewater's mixer levels (config `CfgTidewaterSounds`):

* surf wave by wave on the nearest shores (crash where it breaks, wash, backwash) and the distant surf as
  3D loops on the nearest shore, so the sea is heard from where it is;
* gulls and terns calling over the shore by day;
* the lobster boat's diesel (pitched with rpm, in place of OFP's ship engine), water lapping and rushing
  past the hull, chop slapping it;
* the humpback's blow, breach, splash, fluke-up dive and its song (clear underwater, faint above).
