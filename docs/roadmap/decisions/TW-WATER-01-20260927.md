## Why this base and not `consolidated-v2`

Plan §1 asks for a worktree rebased onto `consolidated-v2`. On the mirror today
`consolidated-v2` was last pushed 2026-09-13 and `codex/streaming-residency` is 293 commits
ahead of it. That includes the 2026-09-19 ocean/surf work the plan's §2 reading list and §3
numbers come from, and the plan itself says its line numbers were read near
`codex/streaming-residency`. Branching from the stale trunk would have dropped all of that,
so W1 is based on `codex/streaming-residency` and nothing was merged into trunk. The owner
decides where it lands.

## What changed

- **`water_backend.rs` (new).** `WaterBackend` owns the active water. `CurrentOp` wraps today's
  `Water` + `Underwater` unchanged, and every Current OP path is a one-line forward.
  - Only one backend exists at a time. A switch drops the old one, builds the new one at the
    frame boundary (`render_frame` entry) and replays the producer's last params, cascade
    configs and interaction params, because the producer resends cascade configs only on change.
  - `SurfaceProduct` is what the rest of the renderer reads: ocean level, local
    level/clock/submersion, `owns_terrain_wetness`, `writes_reactive_mask`,
    `uses_planar_reflection` and `has_underwater`.
- **`lib.rs`.** `water: WaterBackend` replaces the two fields `water: Water` and
  `underwater: Underwater`.
  - The three underwater composites go through `render_underwater`. The froxel and caustics
    block borrows the compositor from the backend.
  - The planar reflection additionally requires `uses_planar_reflection()`.
- **ABI.** `WgrWaterParams` gains `water_backend: u32` (+3 pad) and `tidewater: [vec4; 4]`,
  appended after the body table, so size goes 1056 → 1136 on both sides.
  - No WGSL mirror of `WaterParams` reads past `bodies`, so no lane moved (tested).
  - The layout hash refuses a stale exe/DLL pair; `WGR_ABI_VERSION` is unchanged per the
    header's own rule, since the hash covers size changes.
- **`WGR_WATER_BACKEND=0|1`** overrides the selector for a whole session (harness runs).
- **C++.**
  - `Engine::WaterSettings::waterBackend` and a `tidewater` parameter group.
  - The profile gains `backend` and `tidewater` groups. A profile without them, v4 or v5,
    reads as backend 0 with Tidewater defaults.
  - `WaterWgpu` packs both, including the reference-sea → Tidewater table (see TW-WATER-02).
  - The Water tab gets a **Water backend** combo at the top. "Tidewater Native" is listed but
    disabled in W1: `water_tw::AVAILABLE` is false and the renderer also refuses it.

## Open question from §9, answered (not decided)

**Do inland waters use the same `Water` owner?** Yes. Rivers, lakes and ponds are the WRL-003
body table (`WgrWaterParams::bodies`, `WgrWaterNode::body`), drawn by the same `Water` pipeline
as the sea. Tidewater Native therefore needs a documented fallback for them. Options for the
owner:

1. Tidewater draws the ocean only; bodies are not drawn in Tidewater mode.
2. Tidewater draws bodies as flat Tidewater-material sheets clipped to their ellipse and level.
3. Keep Current OP alive for bodies in Tidewater mode. This breaks §4.2's "the inactive backend
   has no resources".

**Not decided here.** W2 implements option 1 as the interim state and says so in the Water tab.
