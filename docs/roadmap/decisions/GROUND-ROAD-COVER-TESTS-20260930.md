# Ground-road cover and dry shading cost falsifiers

Read-only review of root's road integration `4e4333d1` found two concrete defects. First, dry/master-off ground roads entered the cover branch and evaluated the zenith map's nine depth comparisons even when no film could exist. Second, simply multiplying feathered rain reach by map coverage could paint a sheltered roof edge: fully blocked geometric reach at .1 map validity becomes .9 lighting reach, and the former rain gate then admitted about .0945 wet film.

Root corrected both in `0a5f4b36`: road work is skipped below film wetness activation, shelter sampling happens only after an actual positive mask/land factor, and shared `ground_puddle_rain_factor` removes the lighting map's open fallback before evaluating geometric rain admission. Terrain and roads use the same factor. Physical cover remains independent of artistic ambient strength/floor, and missing/outside coverage remains conservatively dry. Fully disabling the physical directional map therefore suppresses this film; an independent full-world cover cache remains future work.

The new `shaders/ground_road_tests.rs` adds a complete direct shader composition plus source contracts for ground-pinned OnSurface admission, ordinary direct/retained object exclusions, shared snow/land/film factors, simulation-time/liquid-rain ripple lanes and the positive-film-only shelter query. These checks establish source routing rather than runtime draw submission or measured frame-time savings.

Its actual-helper GPU falsifier uses 32 cases: blocked, open, below-threshold and filtered-partial geometric rain reach across eight coverage-validity levels. The inputs include the production lighting map's open feather. A roof must remain exactly dry across the entire border; open land fades with validity; absent physical coverage is zero. A side channel deliberately evaluates the former incorrect formula and must expose a positive covered-edge result, preventing an empty/equivalent fixture from passing unnoticed.

Verification for this new slice is pending root's serial run: `cargo test -p wgpu_renderer ground_road -- --test-threads=1` with established target arguments after registering the module. No production changes, local crate/GPU build, game or deployment were performed by this agent. Fast source assertions, rustfmt parsing and diff checks are the only local checks.

Separately, root's existing combined renderer run recorded **596 passed** in `build/weather-combined-final-tests-2.log`, including the eight-case × two-path actual mud geometry tests. That run predates this new road-edge test and cannot be reported as acceptance of it. Runtime road continuity, real roof shelter and temporal reflection quality still need installed controls; helper readback and source contracts do not establish appearance.
