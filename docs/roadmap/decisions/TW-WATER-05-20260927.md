# TW-WATER-05 — W3c: Tidewater surf-foam look

Work order: *OpenPoseidon × Tidewater — Water Plan v5*, package W3, third step.
Branch: `dec/tidewater-w3-shore`, after W3b (TW-WATER-04).

## What is ported

This was ported source-faithfully from `dgreenheck/tidewater` @ `4811ba48` (MIT) and is credited in
`CREDITS.md`.

**`SurfFoam.js` → `water_tw/tw_surf_foam.wgsl`.** It covers:

- **The lace.** It lives in world space and is carried by the simulated flow with a dual-phase
  flow map (1.2 s cycle, 2.5 m/s cap). On steep faces a vertical projection rolls down the
  face with the roller.
- **Whitewater.** Lumps at ~1.3 m, 0.5 m and 0.2 m, a 3-step sun march for their
  self-shadowing, and sky occlusion in the crevices.
- **Foam carried by the water.** A web of bubble strands that widens into a mat and tears into
  filaments as it thins.
- **Foam lighting.** Wrapped diffuse and the Mikkelsen surface-gradient relief normal, plus
  green-white forward scattering through thin foam and inter-reflection inside the churn.

It is attached with the shore simulation, as Tidewater attaches it (App.js). With the
simulation off the material keeps Tidewater's plain foam path.

## Adaptations to OP (also in the file header)

- **Derivatives.** naga enforces derivative uniformity. Tidewater samples the lace with
  `textureSample` inside the surf branches and differentiates in `surfFoamLight`, which the
  material calls in a branch. OP handles both differently:
  - The lace is sampled with `textureSampleGrad`. The gradient of each pattern coordinate is
    built from the fragment's world-position derivatives, analytically for the warped
    along-crest coordinate.
  - The relief gradients are taken by the material at its top level (`surfFoamGrad`).

  These are the gradients implicit derivatives give, without their spikes at the flow-map
  resets.
- **`shoreDirAt`.** Tidewater's regional direction texture becomes the shore field's direction,
  Tidewater's own fallback. The surf weight uses the coverage of OP's simulated regions.
