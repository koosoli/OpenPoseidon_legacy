# TW-WATER-07 — W3e: the gameplay sea follows Tidewater in Tidewater mode

Branch: `dec/tidewater-w3e-boat-sea`, on top of `dec/tidewater-w3-shore` (a2b9c135).
Status: implemented; measured (see below). Not accepted by owner.

## The report

Dec, 2026-09-27, watching the showcase mission (`dev-missions/tw-showcase.eden`): the small
boats (PBRs, not the LST) "bounce off the water and sometimes tip to the side".

## Cause

Nothing in the Tidewater port touches physics (plan rule 4: rendering never becomes authority);
`git diff 3d720430` on `engine/Poseidon/World` and `engine/Poseidon/Game` is empty. The boats ride
the CPU gameplay predictor, `QueryWaterSurfaceScaled` (a six-band Gerstner sum), which Collisions
feeds into every hull point's `under` (wave buoyancy, `WGR_WAVE_BUOYANCY`).

That predictor scales its bands by the **Current OP** Water-tab amplitude, `waveAmp / 0.40`.
`waveAmp` has defaulted to 1.00 since the renderer's reference re-baseline, so the ratio is 2.5:
bands of 1.1–2.1 m, a significant wave height near 10 m, with no shallow-water fade (the 430 m
and 1300 m bands keep full height in a metre of water). In Tidewater mode none of that sea is
drawn — Tidewater's surface comes from its own spectrum — so the boats heaved and rolled on a
storm that is not on screen, worst in the surf where the landing happens.

Measured on the deployed build (G:\CWA-OLI, a2b9c135), showcase mission, PBR height above the
flat sea sampled every ~0.2 s for mission time 4–45 s (`.tw/test-bob.ps1`):

| | PBR height sd | PBR min / max | max vertical speed |
|---|---|---|---|
| wave buoyancy on (as shipped) | 1.29–1.66 m | −5.7 / +3.6 m | ~10 m/s |
| `WGR_WAVE_BUOYANCY=0` (flat) | 0.08–0.11 m | −0.54 / +0.02 m | ~2 m/s |

## Change

`engine/Poseidon/World/Terrain/WaterSurfaceQuery.cpp` only. When the effective water backend is
Tidewater (the Water tab, or `WGR_WATER_BACKEND`, as in the renderer):

- **Amplitude** follows `TidewaterLook::amplitude` (referenced to 1.0) and the swell-energy lane.
  At the defaults this is the predictor's tuned reference sea, Hs ≈ 4.1 m, which is also close to
  the significant height of Tidewater's default spectrum (local 7 m/s + 0.48 swell: Hs ≈ 3.9 m,
  integrating the two JONSWAP systems of `water_tw/fft.rs`). The swell is ~82 % of that variance
  (m0 ratio 4.64), so swell 0 leaves only the local wind sea.
- **Wavelength** follows `TidewaterLook::cascadeScale`; the Current OP sea-state stretch is not
  applied.
- **Shallow water:** every band fades with depth by Tidewater's own cascade attenuation curve
  (full height below min(40 m, 0.08 L), a small per-cascade floor on the beach), using the
  landscape sea bed, so it stays deterministic and server-safe.

Current OP mode, GL33 fallbacks with Current OP selected, and servers without an engine are
unchanged. It is still the CPU predictor: no GPU texture, crest-for-crest it does not match the
drawn sea (and Tidewater's shore waves stay visual only), but the scale, period and the surf-zone
fade now match the water on screen.

## Not done

- Boat force models (planing lift, righting torque) are out of scope for this order (plan §0/§11).
- A multiplayer client in Tidewater mode and a server/client in Current OP mode now compute
  different gameplay seas. This was already true for any two machines with different Water-tab
  amplitudes (the predictor has followed the local look since before this port); the owner may
  want the gameplay sea state pinned by the mission/server instead.
