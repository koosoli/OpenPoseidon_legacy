# Pre-existing fork faults found during the Sinkhole work — 2026-10-03

Seven faults in Open Poseidon itself, none caused by the Sinkhole (Forest) stream, found while
building and play-testing it or reported by the owner on the side. Each fix sits on its own
branch off `95a9eb1d` (tidewater W12e) in the `diag` worktree, so it can merge without the
Sinkhole stack, and is cherry-picked onto `dec/sinkhole-w1b-cave-lighting`, which is what is
installed in `G:\CWA-OLI` (stamp `ac2a9340`). All captures were made through the `.tw` watcher
with `scripts/with-game-lock.sh`; the harness scripts and missions are untracked under `.tw/`.

Ledger tickets: FIX-FLASH-001, FIX-FLOAT-001, FIX-PILOT-001, FIX-SP3X-001, MAT-NET-001,
MAT-053, FIX-COCKPIT-001.

## 1. Black ground and floating objects on loading frames — FIX-FLASH-001

Owner report: the island goes black and objects float for a moment when loading finishes,
entering/leaving the editor, leaving a preview, and exiting the game.

Cause: GPU-resident (retained) world objects are drawn every frame. The suppress latch was
only set by `World::Simulate`, so `ProgressSystem::Frame` splash frames inherited "draw" and
painted the objects with no terrain under them.

Fix (`dec/fix-retained-world-flash` 5d390885): `render_frame` suppresses world objects on any
frame with no terrain and no 3D draws, and `ProgressSystem::Frame` sets the latch before
`InitDraw`.

## 2. Floating forests and buildings — FIX-FLOAT-001

Owner report: forests and buildings sit up to ~100 m in the air, in missions and editor play.

Causes, all about caches derived from the heightfield outliving a change to it:
- `MakeObjectsTerrainAbsolute` re-seats objects with `SetPosition`, which never cleared the
  cached conform height / bbox level that only `Move` cleared.
- Forests (`ForestPlain`) were skipped by that re-seat entirely, keeping a conform plane and a
  skew baked from the old heights.
- Subdivision cache v1 was accepted whenever land range and grid matched -- which every
  OFP/CWA island shares -- so a cache built from another `eden.wrp` or an older decoder
  replaced the terrain silently. An unnamed world shared one cache file.
- The re-seat looked the ground up past the map edge and put edge-strip objects at -100 m.

Fix (`dec/fix-terrain-change-object-caches` cd12f617, d9a2c402): `Object::OnTerrainChanged`
(forests un-skew and re-skew), called for every object including forests; subdivision cache
v2 stamped with an FNV-1a hash of the source heights and the level, none for an unnamed
world; the height revision bumps in `Landscape::Dim`; the re-seat clamps its lookup into the
map. Verified on Everon (v1 caches rebuilt as v2; forests grounded across
`setTerrainGrid` 50/12.5). Not reproduced: the owner's "buildings float at a decent view
distance" -- needs a location.

## 3. Addon crash on spawn (fml_vorhees) — FIX-PILOT-001

Owner report: the FML Vorhees addon crashes OpenPoseidon or stops the editor previewing.

Cause (symbolised): `Man::CalculateCameraPosition` <- `RecalcPositions` <- `ResetMovement` <-
`Man::Init`. The Jason models' memory LOD has a "pilot" selection with no points; every reader
took `sel[0]`. One reader (the SimProxy eye position) never checked for a missing selection.

Fix (`dec/fix-empty-memory-selection` 5811b896): an empty "pilot" selection is no pilot point
(logged once per type); the unchecked reader checks. Verified: six Jason variants spawn and
live.

## 4. Old-format soldiers torn into spikes — FIX-SP3X-001

Owner report: the Jasons have "weird shapes coming off" them; the same addon is fine in
Malprave and CWA 3.50.

Isolation (captures): Jason's body on the stock moves tears; a stock soldier on Jason's moves
does not; identical on wgpu and GL33, and with the FACE-002 rig and GPU skin baking off.
Malprave sends OFP SP3X MLODs through the original `LODShape` loader and only Arma P3DM
through `ShapeAdapter`; Oli sends every MLOD through `ShapeAdapter`, whose SP3X conversion
AST-018 records as never looked at.

Fix (`dec/fix-sp3x-soft-weights` 5106f39c): MLODs whose every LOD is SP3X load through the
original loader; `POSEIDON_SP3X_ADAPTER=1` restores the adapter. Verified: Jasons render
correctly; the Sinkhole test cave and basement (also SP3X) capture identically through both.
A first attempt that decoded the soft selection byte in the adapter (d35b52ac) changed
nothing and is reverted (637f9edd). Open: the adapter's actual SP3X fault, and
`ObjectStreamPreparer` (modern worlds only) still adapts SP3X.

## 5. Camo nets see-through — MAT-NET-001

Owner report: through the camo netting on Fortress 1/2, the AI soldier on the other side is
transparent.

MAT-NET-001 (netting as a depth-writing cutout) existed and shipped off on 2026-09-07 until
someone looked through a camo net. Captured: an M2 inside a Fortress1 from four sides. Off,
`maskovaci_sit_new` draws as scattered flecks and the gun reads as unobstructed; on, a full
camo cover with the gun only through the real gaps.

Fix (`dec/fix-blend-solid-depth` 738d7ef9): default on; `POSEIDON_NET_DEPTH=0` restores it off.

## 6 and 7. See-through floors (spec_destrb_d) — FIX-COCKPIT-001 and MAT-053

Owner report: `G:\OFP196\Addons\spec_destrb_d.pbo` -- its floors show the storey below.

Reproduced at eye height on each storey (floors mapped by dropping a probe soldier: 30.35,
35.7, 40.33 m ASL). Two faults, and the floors need both fixes:

- **FIX-COCKPIT-001.** `DrawSortObject` called a draw a cockpit when `drawLOD ==
  InsideLOD()`, without checking the object. `Building::InsideLOD` returns 0, so every
  building drawn at LOD 0 -- every building up close -- took the Cockpit* pass family meant
  for the camera vehicle's interior. Instrumented, the ghosting draw carried
  `WGR_DRAW3D_COCKPIT`. Fix (`dec/fix-building-cockpit-route` 6074cfe8): `World` publishes the
  object it queued at its inside LOD this frame (`DrawInsideVehicle`) and `DrawSortObject`
  requires it. Jeep first-person cockpit captured unchanged.
- **MAT-053.** The floor texture `big2c.paa` is 95.4% a=255 but classifies Blend on a 2.9%
  partial strip, so its sections draw depth-test only and a section's far triangles paint
  over its near ones. The engine flags blended, depth-test-only, non-decal draws whose texture
  is at least 75% a=255 (`WGR_DRAW3D_BLEND_SECTION`); the colour pass draws each first with
  depth write keeping only texels at alpha >= 0.98, then normally. `WGR_BLEND_SOLID_DEPTH=0`
  turns it off. Branch `dec/fix-blend-solid-depth` (585f4546, 4662013e, 6596c6aa; trace
  53a300f7).

Record of a mistake: every MAT-053 A/B before the cockpit fix showed no change, because
MAT-053 skips cockpit-flagged draws by design and the building was cockpit-flagged. It was
reverted on that evidence (bacc89ae / d98cb19c) and reapplied once the cockpit fix showed the
floors still ghost without it (c76612d4 / ac2a9340). Captured: cockpit fix + MAT-053 = solid
floors; cockpit fix alone = still ghosted.
