# Experimental natural fine-water ownership

This source slice keeps `POSEIDON_RAIN_WATER_FINE=1` and
`WGR_RAIN_WATER_FINE=1` experimental, with eight total fine tiles. It does not
activate either default, change the renderer ABI/shader, or accept ordinary
all-map water appearance. Full engine compilation, matched installed natural
admission and performance remain pending root verification.

completed normally with exact Noe native source2048/6.25, revision27 admitted1

Previously only genuine HeightChange journal footprints could acquire native
owners, and the source-refresh method returned immediately at an unchanged
height revision. A separate simulation-clock method now ranks retained coarse
runoff without inventing a terrain edit or seeding water. Every five simulation
seconds it scans at most the existing513x513 cached depths, ranks complete
inland8x8-parent tiles by retained volume above the current inland background
mean, and reads at most two33x33 native candidates. One complete owner may be
admitted per cycle. Stable spatial tie-breaking has no time/noise/camera seed.
Peak depth above2mm and excess peak above1mm are scarce-resolution scheduling
policy, not hydrological truth, an optical cutoff or a visibility mask. Missed
scheduling intervals coalesce into one cycle without losing solver pending time. No
stored volume is removed because it falls below these thresholds.

The method requires the exact live owner/range/spacing/height revision already
accepted by the regular source-refresh path. Actual native6.25m/coarse25m,
stride4, zero origin and complete inland source bounds remain mandatory. Other
grid layouts fail closed under the unchanged paired publication contract.
Missing, nonfinite and coastal native candidates are refused and not repeatedly
re-read until source geometry changes. Existing edited owners stay pinned;
observed edits pin overlapping natural owners, and an unobserved revision gap
pins existing natural owners conservatively. Eight wet owners can saturate the
budget and prevent new admission. Global ranking cannot represent every natural
pool, and there is no camera-driven eviction or automatic all-map claim.

On new refinement each coarse parent's retained volume is reconstructed across
its16 actual anti-diagonal child geometries. A monotonic common-head solve uses
their exact submerged-volume integrals, followed by a finite residual assignment
that preserves even sub-ULP mass. High children can remain dry instead of
receiving equal perched water shares. This is local equilibrium reconstruction
inside one25m parent; it does not recover historic disconnected shorelines or
move mass across parents. Existing refined terrain refreshes preserve each
child's own volume. Ordinary finite-volume face flow evolves the result.

Natural owners may retire only after their exact total volume is zero and
they have remained empty for ten actual simulation seconds. Any nonzero
residual, however small, refuses retirement. Parent ownership and the latest
coarse source bed are restored; water budgets and pending solver time stay
intact. The first observed empty tick starts the quiet interval; its elapsed
time cannot prove when a previously wet tile drained. Retirement executes on
the next five-second scheduling cycle after ten seconds of observed emptiness.
Complete child blocks are reused, adjacent empty blocks are joined, and
repeated dry/refine cycles do not append abandoned1024-cell arrays. Zero-owner
paired coarse publication remains valid. Mission reset retires all local state;
regrid clears natural scheduling state with the existing conservative regrid.

No source-receipt trust was weakened. A topology generation change still forces
a real matching heightmap upload in TerrainWgpu. A completed scheduling cycle
publishes one final topology; native2048 uploads cost about16MiB and8192 about
256MiB. No camera scheduling, default enablement, renderer layout expansion or
receipt reuse optimization is included. This cost and the CPU full coarse scan
and head/edge rebuild need actual timing before wider use.

CPU rain remains uniformly applied to inland owners; roof/snow/raised-support
visibility uses the existing physical fragment guards. This candidate does not
claim shelter-aware hydrology, subtract hidden water, change rainfall budgets,
or fill sub-texel Mud/Sand boot deformation. Those require explicit additional
source and budget integration.
