# TW-WATER-06 — W3d: Tidewater breaker lips

Work order: *OpenPoseidon × Tidewater — Water Plan v5*, package W3, fourth step (it completes the W3
port apart from spray, which is W4).
Branch: `dec/tidewater-w3-shore`, after W3c (TW-WATER-05).

## What is ported

This was ported source-faithfully from `dgreenheck/tidewater` @ `4811ba48` (MIT) and is credited in
`CREDITS.md`.

**`Breakers.js` → `water_tw/breakers.rs`, `tw_breakers_kernel.wgsl`, `tw_lip.wgsl`.**

- **Stations** sit every 0.6 m along the shoreline. Each frame a compute pass marches every
  station's transect (64 steps of 2 m) and finds the crests as integer crossings of the
  shore-wave phase, refined by two secant steps. This is the analytic field that drives the
  water surface, so a lip sits exactly on the rendered crest. For each crest it stores the lip
  root, back, breaking progress, height, direction and trough, in one of two slots by wave
  parity.
- **The lip** is a ribbon mesh of 20 profile vertices per station and slot: two on the back of
  the crest and 18 along a ballistic curtain that leaves the crest horizontally and falls in
  front of the concave face.
- **Its material** shades it like the water:
  - Fresnel sky reflection and sun glint, plus turquoise light through the thin sheet;
  - streaks stretched down the jet;
  - a ragged aerated leading edge;
  - once it lands, blotches of whitewater that dissolve into the roller.

  It is blended over the water with premultiplied alpha, depth-tested, and not depth-written.

## Adaptations to OP (also in the file headers)

- **Stations.** Tidewater traces its one beach once: marching north over a fixed x range and
  keeping the longest run. OP does it differently:
  - It traces the sea-level contour inside each shore-simulation region when the region is
    placed, with marching squares on a 1 m grid. That takes about 5 ms for a 380 m region.
  - It keeps every run of at least 30 m, longest first, up to 2048 stations per region.
  - It smooths each run (Tidewater's 30 passes, on both coordinates) and resamples it every
    0.6 m, with the normal pointing to the lower terrain.
  - A crest's id carries its run, so no lip joins two runs.

  - The side toward the sea is decided once per run, by a vote of its stations.
  - Each region's stations are cached with what they were traced from: the centre, the
    terrain generation and the sea level in 0.1 m steps. A slot is re-traced only when one of
    those changes.

  Breakers therefore exist where the shore simulation runs. With the lip opacity at 0 neither
  the crest finder nor the lips run.
- **Occlusion by the wave.** Tidewater's water writes depth, so the wave's back hides the
  curtain from a camera on the sea side. OP's water pass keeps depth read-only for every
  backend. The lip shader therefore hides whatever part of the curtain the sight line reaches
  below the crest line, from the crest record.
- **Light.** The sky reflection is OP's sky/cloud env bake. The sun is OP's (π · sun_diffuse)
  times the cloud shadow, as in Tidewater. OP's aerial perspective is applied.
- **Spray.** The emitters in the kernel and the spray shadow on particles come with the spray
  in W4.
- **Timer.** The crest finder runs inside GPU timer row 80, "Tidewater: shore sim".

## Water tab

- **TW breaker lips** sets the lip opacity (Tidewater `sheet`). The default is 1; 0 turns the
  lips off.
- It is saved as the profile line `tidewaterLips`, in lane `tidewater_shore[2].w`, which was
  already reserved.
