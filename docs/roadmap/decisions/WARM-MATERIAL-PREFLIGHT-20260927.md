# Warm material-stage source preflight experiment

The explicit `WGR_OBJECT_STREAM_WARM_MATERIAL_PREFLIGHT=1` experiment attempted earlier header initialization for missing cached material stages. Actual ON and matched OFF runs both failed their strict upload gates and exited normally. The feature remains OFF. No material-stage performance benefit or readiness acceptance is established.

## Source and ownership contract

Source16ff6aaa permits only already-cached first NormalMap/SpecularDetail paths, under existing warm source/jobs flags and joined owner execution. Existing bank entries, including Unknown provenance, are never reinitialized or relabelled. At most two missing-entry Load attempts per recentre share the existing256 metadata visits; failures and throws consume attempts. This numerical cap is not a time budget: ordinary header Load is synchronous and indivisible. No material parsing, GPU upload, source-cap increase, worker bank access or texture destruction is introduced.

Ordinary bank ownership remains intact; a temporary Ref keeps the returned Texture alive through inspection. Only actual initialized source/key facts may reach unchanged source-validated worker publication and upload gates. A returned Texture does not prove successful Init or readiness, and failed ordinary bank entries may persist. OFF warm flags avoid this path; signature/argument changes and a warm-only stack counter are not literal zero footprint. No new instance fields or Rust ABI are introduced.

The runner requires an accepted preflight source joined canonically to an actual successful source-validated bound worker stage-family upload, in addition to existing independent warm pipeline gates. Missing witness writes passedfalse and retained evidence before NotExercised. Filename/source association does not prove shader-slot role, pixels, complete material readiness, source freshness or causal timing.
