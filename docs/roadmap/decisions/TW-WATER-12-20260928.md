## W4 — what shipped and what did not

* W4a spray (TW-WATER-10), W4a.1–3 fixes and diagnostics, W4b lips + spray in the reactive mask.
* **RefractionPass: deferred.** Tidewater renders the scene below the sea level a second time
  (half resolution, a guard band, fragments above sea level + 0.4 m discarded) because its water
  used to fake the seabed from nearby pixels behind piers and its boat, which smeared. OP's water
  already falls back to the unrefracted pixel wherever the refracted lookup would land on
  something in front of the surface (tw_water.wgsl, `valid`), which cannot smear; the cost of the
  pass would be a second terrain + object render every frame. The showcase landing (PBRs beached
  in the shallows, fe060939) shows no smearing or ghosting at the hulls. Revisit if piers or hulls
  seen up close show the refraction step at their edges.

## W5a — underwater composite

Tidewater's `post/Underwater.js` composite, in OP's backend underwater slot (the renderer's
existing compositor engagement: Water tab underwater checkbox, camera within the engage band or
submerged): the medium at the lens and the waterline split, absorption + single-scattered sun and
sky light, the meniscus band. See `water_tw/underwater.rs` and `tw_underwater.wgsl` for the OP
differences (per-pixel medium, FFT-only water height, submerged scene light attenuated in post as
Tidewater's UnderwaterLighting does, in-scatter at the water glow but at least 0.12). Not yet:
caustics on the seabed and the caustic light shafts / diver's torch (W5b).
