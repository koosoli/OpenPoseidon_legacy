# TW-WATER-09 — W3g: Tidewater's sea conditions (wave height and presets)

Branch: `dec/tidewater-w3g-sea`, on top of `dec/tidewater-w3f-colour`.
Status: implemented. Visual check and owner acceptance pending.

## The report

Oli, 2026-09-27: "make sure that wave height and presets are similar to the original Tidewater
game or our previous implementation."

## Before

Tidewater mode took only the wind from OP's weather. Everything else that sets Tidewater's sea
state stayed at its Breezy defaults whatever the weather: fetch 120 km, chop 0.9, swell 0.48,
surf 0.34 m at 9 s, whitecaps 0.5. A calm or stormy day therefore got Breezy's swell and surf
with a different wind sea on top, a combination Tidewater never shows. The logged weather wind in
the showcase (07:30, overcast 0.6) was 6.0 m/s, close to Breezy, which is why the default day
looked about right.

## Change

Tidewater's own preset table (`src/ui/AppUI.js`, `SEA`) now drives the sea state:

| Conditions | wind m/s | fetch km | chop | swell | surf m | period s | whitecaps |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Calm | 3.5 | 40 | 0.75 | 0.28 | 0.18 | 11 | 0.2 |
| Breezy (Tidewater default) | 7 | 120 | 0.9 | 0.48 | 0.34 | 9 | 0.5 |
| Choppy | 12 | 300 | 1.05 | 0.68 | 0.56 | 8.5 | 0.75 |
| Storm | 20 | 900 | 1.2 | 1.0 | 0.9 | 12 | 1 |

- **TW sea conditions** (Water tab): *Weather* (default) interpolates the table at the latched
  weather wind, so a 7 m/s day is exactly Breezy and every lane moves together as it does in
  Tidewater; *Calm / Breezy / Choppy / Storm* apply the preset including its wind; *Custom* is the
  old behaviour (weather wind, the sliders as set).
- Whitecaps set the Jacobian foam threshold as Tidewater's `whitecaps()` does
  (0.5 + 0.16 w + 0.01 clamp(U − 7, −5, 12)); OceanFFT's decay 0.35 is kept.
- The preset surf is pre-divided by the renderer's swell / 0.48 surf scaling, so the net surf is
  the preset's. Current OP's reference seas (Cascade Preset 3/4/5) still apply their multipliers on
  top, as documented in TW-WATER-02.
- ABI: `WgrWaterParams::tidewater_sea` (wind override, fetch, whitecaps, mode); sizeof 1184 → 1200,
  layout hash refuses a stale pair.
- Profiles: a `tidewaterSea` line. A profile without it gets *Weather*; users who had tuned the
  chop / swell / surf sliders pick *Custom* to get them back.

Tidewater's panel quotes the local wind sea as Hs ≈ 0.0246 U²: 0.3 m Calm, 1.2 m Breezy,
3.5 m Choppy, 9.8 m Storm (plus the swell).
