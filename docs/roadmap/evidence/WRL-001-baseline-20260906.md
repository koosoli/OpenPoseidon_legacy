## 4. Performance contract frozen for the branch

- Percentage gate: 5% of the median GPU frame in `perf_water.eden` = 1.5 ms. Absolute allowance frozen at the tighter of that and **1.0 ms** on the sum of the water rows (`Water draw` + FFT + foam + interaction + planar + underwater rows), all measured on the same installed pair, VSync off, identical `graphics.cfg`.
- CPU: 5% of 29.7 ms = 1.5 ms on `frame_avg`, matched scenes.
- Memory caps (established from the allocation inventory, section 5): no new persistent render target for milestone A; milestone B may add at most one 8-bit water-mask target at render resolution (≈2.2 MB at 2305x965) and a per-body metadata buffer bounded at 64 bodies x 96 B. Reflection stays at one planar view per frame. FFT stays 512 with the reference 3-cascade preset.

## 5. Production-path inventory (verified on this branch)

Surface: `engine/WgpuRenderer/rust/src/water/water.wgsl` `fs_water`; CDLOD submission `engine/WgpuRenderer/WaterWgpu.cpp` `DrawWater`; params applied at `EngineWgpu::ApplyPendingRendererSettings` (the final settings application point; env toggles are overwritten by this per-frame push). FFT 512, `cascadePreset=1` (88/57/16 m, layer 3 disabled), spectrum re-derived only on latched wind buckets (20 s dwell, 1 m/s and 15 degree buckets). Planar: one view per frame, coverage gate cut 0.5% / full 6%, divisor 2..6 with a 20-frame hold, FOV pad 1.35. SSR: 20 steps in-shader within 320 m. Refraction: opaque snapshot sampled with a distance-validated offset. Underwater: compositor + froxel + caustics, **off by default** (`underwaterEffect=false`). Temporal: DLSS in the owner's config, reactive-water mask lane exists.

Allocation inventory (persistent): FFT displacement/dynamics/auxiliary 512x512x4 rgba16f x3 (~24 MB), spectrum packs, foam history 1024x1024 rgba16f x2 (16 MB), interaction 256x256 x2, planar colour + mip chain at render/div, froxel 32x18x32, caustics 128x128, water visibility query ring. Nothing here scales with body count today because there is exactly one body.

Body-data availability report: stock OFP/CWA worlds (Everon, Malden, Kolgujev, Nogova) carry **no inland water bodies** at all; water is the single tidal sea plane (`Landscape::GetSeaLevel`, `_seaLevelWave`). Imported worlds (DayZ Enoch/Sakhal, Arma 3, Reforger) carry ponds and rivers only as `CalmWater` model surfaces: reflective flag, world-space ripple, no elevation/extent/flow metadata reaching the renderer, degenerate UVs (DZ-005), and no closed-volume information (DZ-008). Rivers' `WaterStreamMap` is never read. **A bounded elevated lake and a sloping river must therefore be synthetic fixtures**, declared as such (plan section 5, WRL-003 step 2), until an importer supplies body metadata.

Gameplay coupling confirmed: `QueryWaterSurface` reads `waveAmp`, `waveSpeed`, `waveScale`, `seaStateCoupling`; its unit test pins none of the numeric values. `waveAmp` defaults to 1.00 while both the shader's sea-state coupling and the CPU predictor treat 0.40 as the reference, so the shipped default already runs the coupling at 2.5x (recorded, not changed).

## 6. Ranked visible problems (author's reading of the baseline captures)

1. **Eye-level open sea is a flat, saturated ultramarine with almost no sky in it** (`sea.png`): at a 4 degree view angle the sea should be dominated by the reflected sky; here the body colour wins. Related knobs: `reflectionGain` 0.7, the 0.7 Smith blend in `env_visibility`, and the body radiance magnitude. Optical, not spectral.
2. **Round white spray sprites float on a calm sea** (`sea.png`, wind 5 m/s, sea-state lane 0.28): the whitewater billboards read as snowflakes, not spindrift. Gate: `rifleImpactSpray` / `waterSplashParticleActivity` 0.25.
3. **Aerial view is a uniform blue with a light "lace" of foam patches over the whole surface** (`seaair.png`): the plan's "persistent gritty coverage over the entire sea". Sources: persistent breaker foam + the 2.7x structure noise; `waveFoamDeepFalloff` is already 1.0, so the remaining coverage is the crest/jacobian term plus history.
4. **The transmitted seabed in the bay reads as a hard turquoise band with a soft grey rim** (`coast.png`, `bay.png`): plausible, but its extinction is a different curve from the one the underwater volume uses, so above/below do not match (fixed by WRL-002).
5. **Sunset/night**: not assessable from the 19.5 h shot (night); needs a 17.5 h capture.

Not defects but recorded: the horizon band of clouds is washed by the sky pass, not water; the shoreline foam is a thin line at this distance (the coast-distance far fade is doing what it was tuned to do).
