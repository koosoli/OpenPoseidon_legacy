## 1. What will mislead you — read first

### Most `WGR_*` gates are inert

C++ rebuilds and pushes the settings structs **every frame**, so an env var read
once at construction is overwritten immediately. Confirmed inert: `WGR_GRASS`,
`WGR_GRASS_DENSITY`, `WGR_GRASS_DISTANCE`, `WGR_SKY_VIS*`. `sky_vis.rs` says so in
a comment — *"C++ pushes every frame and wins, so this only decides frame 0"*.

A gate that silently reverts is worse than no gate: it yields a plausible number
for the wrong configuration. **`WGR_ABLATE=grass,ao,skyvis,planar,clouds` applies
at the push site** and is the one to use. It shouts about unknown tokens.

Structurally safe (read once into a member, or a pipeline override constant):
`WGR_GPU_DRIVEN`, `WGR_MSAA`, `WGR_HDR`, `WGR_PREPASS`, `WGR_INDIRECT`,
`WGR_GODRAYS`, `WGR_TERRAIN_SKIRT_K`, `WGR_MATERIAL_DEBUG_*`, `WGR_INSTANCE_TRACE`.

### Low variance is NOT evidence of a valid measurement

I measured 174 ms against an earlier 34 ms with a **tight 4% spread** and was about
to report a 5× regression. Bisecting — stashing every uncommitted change and
rebuilding — gave **190–239 ms on the same commit** that had measured 30–48 ms an
hour earlier. It was never the code. A runaway `find` of mine had been burning CPU
for 18,000 s, and DaVinci Resolve (GPU-heavy) was running.

A consistently degraded machine gives consistently slow numbers. **Only
comparisons interleaved within one batch survive this**, because both arms then see
the same machine state. Check for stray processes and GPU-using applications
before quoting any absolute frame time.

### An instrument that mutates what it measures

Setting `WGR_MATERIAL_DEBUG_RVMAT` at all used to disable production material
binding for the whole CPU direct path. Fixed for `__trace__` (now an observer) but
**still live for `*`**, which sets `diagnosticSelector` and kills production
binding. `__unassigned__` and substring selectors also divert matching objects off
the retained path — so they change two things at once. This invalidated one of my
wedge readings and produced a confident wrong identification.

### The capture harness

`cap.ps1` in the session scratchpad. Two things it had to learn the hard way:

- **`PoseidonGame.exe` is a GUI-subsystem binary**, so `& $exe` returns immediately
  and `$LASTEXITCODE` is never set. Launching in a loop starts every run at once and
  they fight over the GPU. **`Start-Process -Wait` is the only form that blocks.**
- I added `-Width`/`-Height` parameters and **never appended them to the command
  line**, so every "1080p" capture silently ran at 800×600. That cost a wrongly
  reverted change. Fixed, but check any conclusion drawn from a resolution pair
  before that fix.

Also: `--screenshot-delay` defaults to **10 frames**, which measures a cold machine
mid-load. Use **300**. And only `GPU frame total` is re-measured per frame; every
other region reports its last harvested value.

## 2. Not finished — in priority order

### 2.1 Arma 3 performance (the owner's top complaint, three times)

**Measured properly once**, interleaved, 6 arms × 3 rounds, 1080p, quiet machine:

| arm | median | vs base |
|---|---:|---|
| base | **34.36 ms** | — |
| `WGR_ABLATE=planar` | 26.42 | **−7.94 ms (23%)** |
| `WGR_ABLATE=ao` | 31.37 | −2.99 |
| `WGR_ABLATE=clouds` | 32.31 | −2.05 |
| `WGR_MSAA=1` | 67.41 | **+33.05** |

34 ms at 1080p is under 30 fps. The planar water reflection is the one item large
enough to matter alone.

**Landed since:** a coverage gate (skip below 0.5% water coverage, resolution ramp
∝ 1/√coverage above it) and half-res GTAO by default. **Not yet measured with a
quiet machine** — that is the first thing to do.

**Known:** water covers **6.50%** at the owner's Stratis camera, just above the 6%
`full` threshold, so the shipped defaults leave it at full quality and save nothing
*there*. `WGR_PLANAR=0.005,0.30,2,6` forces divisor 4 and was measured (interleaved,
so valid) to cut `Planar: terrain` 11.87→4.40, `sky` 8.71→1.65, `clouds` 6.45→1.93.
**That is a quality decision — 35% of pixels changed. The owner has not seen it.**

**Open and worth chasing:** `WGR_MSAA=1` measuring **33 ms slower** than 4× is not
plausible as a real cost. The performance agent's hypothesis, with code citations:
at 1 sample the planar reflection renders **directly into mip 0 of a mipped,
TEXTURE_BINDING texture** (`ensure_planar_target` picks `mip_views[0]` instead of a
dedicated attachment-only target), which likely disables framebuffer compression
and forces a decompress per mip transition. Decisive test: `WGR_MSAA=1
WGR_ABLATE=planar` vs `WGR_MSAA=4 WGR_ABLATE=planar` — if they match, the pathology
is entirely in the 1× planar target.

**Also open:** no measured cost for grass. It is ~2.6 ms of a 34 ms frame — 4% —
which frame-time ablation cannot resolve against the observed spread. Use the GPU
timer regions (`GrassPlaceNear`, `GrassShadow`, the draw rows) instead.

### 2.3 The Arma 2 road banding — nine hypotheses dead

`docs/roadmap/decisions/WLD-021-*.md` has the full trail. **GL33 is clean on
identical data**, so it is wgpu-side. Dead: decal bias (`WGR_DECAL_SCALE=0` moves
road pixels ≤2/255), z-epsilons (fixed to GL33 parity, no change), mirrored
placements (all 438,906 determinants positive, road `up` exactly (0,1,0)), normals
(runtime `Shape::Norm()` is −Y, negated to +Y, sun term correct), the
two-lighting-path fork, prepass, MSAA, GPU terrain conform, terrain LOD/morph.

Both trapezoids are **road** — with `WGR_TERRAIN_A3_SURFACES=0` the terrain moves
20/255 while both road tones move ≤5. It is per-**draw** shading. The roads agent's
next instrument (unapplied, in its last report) re-keys `WGR_ROAD_TRACE` on the
shape pointer and adds `_meshSpec`, the model-to-world translation and determinant,
`_buffer` presence, and a mirror probe in `ShapeDraw.cpp` for shapes that never
reach `DrawSectionTL`.

### 2.4 The orbs — diagnosed, fix gated, look call outstanding

**They are authored yellow lichen on Stratis breakwater rock.** Illumination is
neutral and identical to the bare rock beside them (`lit ≈ 8`); the colour is the
texture's. The per-channel Hable curve at exposure 3.625 compresses R and G onto
the shoulder while B stays below it, manufacturing a saturated ball where film
would give a white highlight.

`WGR_TONEMAP_DESAT=<start>,<strength>` implements highlight roll-to-white,
**default off**. At 0.5: orb blue 141.6 → 198.9 (+57), bare rock +1.9, hillside
untouched — 30:1 selectivity. **The owner has not chosen.** Send him 0 / 0.35 / 0.5
at the 16:00 camera (28 blobs there vs 13 at noon).

Deliberately deferred: moving saturation ahead of the curve is worth only ~2/255
and all six presets' saturation values would need re-tuning. Note contrast is
identically 1.0 and lift identically 0.0 in every preset — dead code.

## 3. What landed (so you do not re-derive it)

Full reasoning is in the commit messages — they are long on purpose.

- **Named selections read indices as byte offsets, on every ODOL revision.**
  373,868 face entries; the old conversion recovered 41,058, of which **40,597
  pointed at the wrong face**. Silent corruption, not just loss. Affects rev 73
  identically, so Arma 3 too. Broke proxy hiding, `componentNN` destruction
  geometry, hit points, damage zones, animation sources.
- **Grass placement** now driven by the map's decoded mask, not a texture-name
  string match applied per 32 m land cell. Stratis surface cells 409,280 → 934,937.
- **The map's own clutter draws** — Takistan 25 cards, Stratis 31, with per-surface
  density from the map's config (field 0.95 vs desert 0.12).
- **A one-character path bug** meant *zero* Arma configs ever parsed (`QFBank`
  prefixes carry a trailing separator).
- **Arma 1 worlds load and are textured.** `Terrain<N>` is a four-bit **slot
  presence mask** — 41% of Sahrani tiles have a hole, so reading groups densely puts
  the wrong surface under the wrong mask channel.
- **The planar reflection was rendering with the main camera's rays** — one uniform
  buffer written twice per frame, last write wins.
- **Cloud-shadow flicker** — the mapping published to samplers was one frame older
  than the map's contents.
- **Vegetation pop-in** — objects culled at 2/3 view distance while fog is anchored
  at full.
- **Mid-distance ground** — detail faded out at 25–120 m against a 1.03 m/texel
  satellite; now 600–900 m.
- **Ballistics diagnostic tab + pause with working freefly.**
- **Moon with a real ephemeris**, verified against Meeus to 0.015°.
- **One wind model** for grass, smoke, clouds, ballistics and ocean.

## 5. Working notes

Nine agents ran in parallel and it worked, with caveats:

- **Serialise contended files through one owner.** `DebugOverlay.cpp` had three
  claimants; routing every change through diffs avoided corruption. Two agents ran
  `git commit -a`-shaped sweeps and swept up others' in-flight edits — one of those
  committed a broken intermediate `ShaderSchema.hpp` that would have bound normal
  maps into Arma 2/3 colour slots. **Check `git diff` before committing on a shared
  tree.**
- **A red tree blocks everyone.** Fix or revert immediately; do not wait.
- **Predictions with a quantitative shape beat presence/absence tests.** The skirt
  hypothesis died because it predicted the artifact height would scale *linearly*
  with `WGR_TERRAIN_SKIRT_K`; presence/absence alone would have been a coincidence
  away from a wrong answer. Always check the ablation was non-vacuous — 27,795
  other pixels moved, so the variable really did reach the pipeline.
- **Agents that refuse the easy answer are the valuable ones.** The grass agent
  rejected a 97% vertex saving because it would have broken bend-under-player, and
  killed two of its own three "lossless" leads with arithmetic. The materials agent
  refused to judge a numeric question by eye and built a `maxFace`/`bigFaces`
  instrument instead, which found the answer in one run.
- The account session limit is real and stops every agent at once. Everything is
  committed and pushed, so nothing was lost, but budget for it.
