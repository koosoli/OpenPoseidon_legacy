# Sinkhole test content: underground test cave and basement

Opt-in test models for terrain holes and cave lighting:
* terrain holes: `WLD-HOLE-001`, `design notes`;
* cave lighting: `LGT-CAVE-001`, `SINKHOLE-01b-cave-lighting-20261003.md`.

They are not shipped or installed by default.

| Folder | Add-on (prefix) | Class | What |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ugcave/` | `ugcave` | `UgCave` | A 24 × 18 m chamber 5 m under the ground, 4.6 m high, with three rock pillars, three boulders and a ledge. An open stairwell leads up to the north, and a 2.4 m tunnel (south, then east) ends in an open ramp up in the east. Four terrain-hole areas; procedural textures (`cave_*.dds`). |
| `ugtest/` | `ugtest` | `UgBasement` | A 6 × 6 m cellar 3 m down under a roof slab at ground level (roadway on top), with a stair up to the north. Visual, geometry, fire geometry, memory (`terrain_hole1/2`), roadway and paths LODs. |
| `tools/` | — | — | The generators (Python 3 + NumPy): `build_cave.py`, `build_ug.py`, `mlod.py`, `make_tex.py`, `preview.py`, `wrp2.py`; rebuild steps in `tools/README.txt`. |

## Use

```powershell
./scripts/Prepare-SinkholeTestMod.ps1   # -> build/sinkhole-test/@OP_SinkholeTest/AddOns/ugcave.pbo, ugtest.pbo
```

Then start the game with `--mod <that folder>` and play one of the missions:
* **`dev-missions/ugcave-test.Eden`:** the cave on the flat ground east of the Everon airfield, with an East team
  inside. Radio Alpha: East weapons free. Bravo: East leaves by the tunnel. Charlie: East goes back in. Delta: your
  squad walks into the cave.
* **`dev-missions/ugbasement-test.Eden`:** the basement at 9698,1622; two soldiers walk down into the cellar.

Copy a mission into the game's `Missions` folder, or run it with `--test-mission`.
